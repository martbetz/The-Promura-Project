Promura-Gekkor was a specific badge and product line designation used on a select tier of vintage manual-focus lenses sold under the Promura brand during the 1970s and 1980s.  

### ​Primary Links & Characteristics

- **​The Name**: "Gekkor" (derived from the Japanese word Gekkō, meaning "moonlight") was frequently used by Japanese optical companies and third-party distributors as a poetic sub-brand or modifier for faster, higher-end lenses—such as the Promura-Gekkor MC 35mm f/1.8 and the Auto Promura-Gekkor 135mm f/1.8  

- **​Shared OEM Production**: while Promura acted as the marketing and distribution label, lenses bearing the "Promura-Gekkor" name were built by Japanese Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs). For example, the fast Promura-Gekkor 135mm f/1.8 is widely documented by vintage lens collectors as having been manufactured by Mitake (and shared identical optics with versions sold under other third-party names like Spiratone, Unitor, and Weltblick)

- **​Market Tiering**: adding "Gekkor" to the lens barrel generally indicated a premium or faster optical formula within Promura's catalog compared to their standard consumer-tier variable zooms or slower kit lenses
