### Overview of Nissin Koki Co. Ltd.

Nissin Koki Co. Ltd. was a Japanese optical and precision manufacturing company that operated primarily as an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) during the vintage lens boom of the 1970s and 1980s. 

Like many third-party Japanese optical houses of that era, Nissin Koki manufactured interchangeable camera lenses (as well as projection optics, and mechanical parts) that were rebranded and sold globally under various independent labels.

#### ​Core Role in Photography & Optics

##### ​Third-Party OEM Production

Nissin Koki supplied lenses to numerous independent marketing brands that didn't have their own manufacturing plants. Nissin Koki are most notably documented by vintage lens collectors as the manufacturer behind Promura lenses (such as the Auto-Promura 28mm and 20mm C.P. Hi-Lux MC series).  

##### ​Projector Lenses

Nissin Koki also produced high-speed projection optics, widely recognized under names like the Nissin Sankor projector lens series, which are popular today among filmmakers for vintage projection-lens adaptations.  

##### ​Market Position

During the mechanical SLR era, Nissin Koki sat alongside other lesser-known independent Japanese manufacturers (such as Mitake, Sun, and Komine) that provided affordable alternatives to first-party camera brand lenses.

#### ​International Expansion and Evolution

#### ​Taiwan Nissin Koki (Zussin Technology)

In November 1976, Nissin Koki established a major joint venture in Taiwan (Taiwan Nissin Koki Co., Ltd.).  

##### ​Transition to Precision Plastics & Electronics

Over the decades, the Taiwan branch evolved significantly; it moved from metal tooling and injection molding into high-precision plastic optics, camera viewfinder lenses for brands like Canon and Konica, smartphone components, and medical optical systems.  

##### ​Corporate Shifts

The Taiwanese entity later associated briefly with the Nidec Group in the 2000s before returning to independent local operation as Zussin Technology.
